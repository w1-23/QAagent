## 定位（应当贯穿摘要、方法、结果）
CitrusScholar: An Integrated AI-Assisted Research Workbench for Citrus Science。贡献是将柑橘研究常见的多源证据查询、原文阅读、引用核验、模拟数据审查及可验证科研成果整合于一体化工作流。**不**声称创新LLM、RAG检索算法、意图分类器、自动科研发现或科学因果判定。现有单 General Agent、17工具与5个登记共享语料的工程实现应保留。网络/本地开关只表示受控资料范围；有确切检索模式约束才评分路由。

## 预注册核心/补充终点与约束
- Core E01 confirmatory system outcome: **`egts_system_outcome`** over all 60
  planned slots. Two domain experts independently score all 180 responses and
  a third expert adjudicates disputed fields; `human_consensus_egts` is
  reported separately with semantic-scoring coverage and infrastructure
  failures. Three candidate LLM judges on one service entry point provide a
  separate automated measurement.
- Supplementary E01 outcome: **target chunk citation hit** across 60 single-chunk-anchored questions, 12 per public corpus; B2-vs-B1 per-question paired difference is reported within this supplementary experiment.
- All 120 formal questions receive two independent human validity certifications; reviewers may be trained graduate students. No Gold Answer set is created.
- Semantic support (`1/0.5/0`) is not reported. System-task outcomes,
  human semantic consensus, machine-judge results and supplementary
  deterministic locator outcomes are reported separately and never pooled.
  The only primary confirmatory comparison is B2 vs B1 on
  `egts_system_outcome`.
- Tool routing: single-intent 8 classes×24=192. Since V15 has request constraints + runtime tool exposure, not an independently exposed intent classifier, report **observed route compliance**. Only if code genuinely exposes predictions may report classifier accuracy with separate ground-truth class and confusion matrix.
- Composite intent: 24 cases report task-set precision/recall/F1 and constraint violations, **not** artificially convert to single class.
- End-to-end: seven cases; results are real task completion with audited artifact SHA, never an inflated 7-item p-value.
- All experiments use fixed data/versions and transparent failures; forbidden
  to fabricate results, silently remove hard questions post hoc, retune on the
  held-out citation60, pass frozen chunks or target locators to runtime,
  conflate unavailable service with an intent mistake, or publicly
  redistribute unauthorized source PDFs. `L0` is excluded, `L1` remains in
  protected server storage, and `L2` is release-checked.

## Pre-declared analyses
Core QA60 semantic outcomes are deterministic aggregation of two expert score
sheets plus field-level third-expert adjudication; report raw agreement,
Gwet's AC1 (primary), Cohen's kappa (supplementary), and provisional EGTS
agreement. The unique confirmatory comparison is B2 vs B1 on
`egts_system_outcome` using two-sided exact McNemar and a 10,000-replicate L/V/W/X/P/N
stratified paired bootstrap, seed `20261104`, retaining B0/B1/B2 for each
resampled question. Infrastructure failures count as system-task failures in
the fixed 60-slot denominator; semantic rates use only valid double-scored
records and always report coverage. B1/B2 vs B0 are descriptive only. J1/J2
score independently and J3 handles four-field disagreements; report LLM-LLM
and LLM-human consensus agreement separately. Supplementary citation60 reports
B2-B1 paired target-chunk citation hit, corpus-stratified paired bootstrap,
descriptive exact McNemar statistics, five-corpus Wilson intervals, and
secondary target-document/allowed-corpus/no-citation/wrong-corpus outcomes.
Both E01 analyses keep failures and environment-unavailable records and use
independent strict-human, strict-judge, strict-citation, and strict-all gates.
No multiple testing to justify isolated cherry-picked wins. 60+60/192 are
planning sizes, not an a priori powered superiority trial.

## HAIant: what to borrow vs not borrow
HAIant (智然体), Molecular Plant, published online 2026-09-09, DOI `10.1016/j.molp.2026.08.011`, positioned a human-centered integrated biological research platform; describes BioSkills 84 analysis tools, personal research records, and demonstrative workflows. A secondary article discusses 8 intent categories and performance figures; **do not quote its numeric results as primary verified until reading and archiving original supplementary methods/results**. What we borrow: evaluation **story rhythm** (intent routing is measurable + end-to-end cases + domain usefulness); **not** its local privacy architecture, personal agent identity or BioSkills scale.
