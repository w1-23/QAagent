## Interpretation (evidence-backed, no inference beyond measurement)
- Each record carries 'sampled_at_utc', a wall-clock timestamp.
- Two runs therefore differ byte-wise, and even timestamp-stripped SHAs
  differ because the canonicalisation used differs from the writer's.
- The 60-byte size delta equals 60 lines x 1 byte, consistent with a
  same-length timestamp shift, not a different chunk set.
- Therefore the frozen 60-chunk SET can be reproduced; the exact
  byte-identical FILE from the manifest cannot, because its SHA binds a
  wall-clock value that is not recorded in the manifest.

## Consequence for the paper
- Citation60 can be REBUILT (selection is stable) but it will not hash-match
  the historical manifest. That is a new version with a new SHA, requiring
  a change_log row and re-freeze under review. It must NOT be presented as
  the original frozen asset.
- The 60 frozen QUESTIONS were authored FROM the original chunk texts and
  are NOT reconstructable: they encode human authoring decisions.
