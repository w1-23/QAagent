**Fig. 4. Web-source mixing risks and structural verification of separated output in CitrusQA.**

**(a)** Judge-flagged hallucination rates under five evidence-handling conditions in the legacy mixed-arbitration architecture. A was the local-only control, with web retrieval disabled; B, C, C′ and D used unfiltered mixing, receipt-level filtering, model-visible filtering and excerpt-only filtering, respectively. The same 48 questions were assigned to two LLM judges in each condition. Bars show the frozen pooled rates of 3.1%, 24.0%, 33.3%, 18.8% and 22.9%; counts are flagged events over the frozen nominal denominator of 96 judge slots. The archived Kimi judgment for one D-arm question is empty, leaving 95 non-empty D-arm verdicts. The specified frozen value of 22/96 is retained here without imputing that missing judgment; it must not be described as 96 valid verdicts. Evaluation was limited by the availability and length of evidence previews supplied to the judges. These findings motivated the later redesign but do not measure its effect on hallucination rates. No confidence intervals or significance tests are displayed.

**(b)** Deterministic structural validation of the separated-output architecture in the v11 evaluation. In the 144-response three-pool batch, zero web citation occurrences were found in local-answer sections and 820 were found in web sections. The left composition strip shows allocation of these occurrences; the two rows below report answers with local-section violations (0/144) and Pool C answers containing web citations (47/48). The latter describes citation use, not an isolation success rate. In a separate 84-question system-level batch, all 1,649 in-text local citation-marker occurrences resolved to sources in the same answer's cited pool, with zero out-of-pool occurrences. Right-hand strips show resolvable and out-of-pool counts against the common denominator of 1,649 markers; the empty baseline represents the zero numerator. Occurrence-level, answer-level and marker-level measurements remain separate. These audits establish citation placement and structural traceability, not semantic support for claims. The two panels use different experimental batches and do not estimate a causal effect of the architecture change.
